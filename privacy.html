Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Denali Equity Investments ("Denali," "we," "us") operates Unlock Private Wealth as an investor-education and relationship-building experience. This policy explains the information we collect through this site and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request The Private Wealth Blueprint, we collect the information you choose to provide, including your first name, last name, and email address. If you separately choose to receive Weekly Market Intelligence, we record that consent. We may also record the page you used, referral source, campaign or UTM information, and ordinary technical information generated when the site and its service providers process your request.

How we use information

We use this information to deliver requested educational materials, send communications you specifically request, maintain records of consent, understand how visitors reach Denali, operate and secure the site, and support Denali's investor-education process.

Email choices

Delivery of the Blueprint is separate from optional ongoing market communications. If you opt in to ongoing email, you may unsubscribe using the instructions in those messages or contact Denali directly.

Service providers

Denali uses service providers to operate this experience, including infrastructure used for database processing and email delivery. Those providers process information on Denali's behalf for the purposes described above.

Sale of personal information

Denali does not sell personal information collected through this site to advertisers or data brokers.

Investor portal credentials

Denali does not collect or store passwords for its third-party investor portal through Unlock Private Wealth.

Retention and security

We retain information for legitimate business, compliance, relationship-management, and recordkeeping purposes and use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect it. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may contact Denali to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Residents of jurisdictions that provide additional privacy rights may exercise those rights through the same contact channel.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to info@denaliequities.com.
