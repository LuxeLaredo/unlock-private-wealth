Investment Disclosures

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Unlock Private Wealth and The Private Wealth Blueprint are educational resources of Denali Equity Investments. They are intended to explain investment concepts and Denali's general approach to disciplined real-estate ownership.

No offer or solicitation

This material does not constitute an offer to sell securities or a solicitation to buy securities. Any investment opportunity will be made only through formal offering documents, including the disclosures, risk factors, subscription materials, and other documents applicable to that specific offering.

Investment risk

Private real estate investments involve substantial risk, including possible loss of principal, illiquidity, changing financing conditions, operating risk, market risk, tenant and occupancy risk, property-level expenses, insurance and tax changes, and risks that may not be known when an investment is made.

Targets, projections, and examples

Any target, projection, illustration, scenario, or example is provided for educational purposes and is not guaranteed. Actual results may differ materially because of acquisition price, financing, operations, market conditions, capital needs, taxes, timing, and exit conditions.

Tax matters

Tax outcomes depend on individual circumstances and applicable law. Depreciation, cost segregation, and other tax considerations may be subject to property type, timing, investor status, income characterization, and current tax rules. Investors should review their own circumstances with qualified tax and legal advisers.

Independent review

Prospective investors should review all formal offering materials, conduct their own due diligence, and consult their CPA, attorney, and financial adviser before making an investment decision.
